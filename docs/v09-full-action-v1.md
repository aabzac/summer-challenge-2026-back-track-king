## Completed results — 2026-10-11

**All967 positions /39 games completed**, with exact native selection and final
fill reconstructed. There were no unsupported roots or incomplete trajectories.
The selected root was a network root in183 positions (ten of the old39 subgroup).
Its own target nomination differed from the pinned WAIT-root nomination in ten
positions; the post-construction chooser differed in15. These are retained
distinctions, not silently regenerated targets. Both alternatives kept the exact
full emitted construction, including any collateral exposure to the changed hit.

### Complete construction preserves most of the old mechanism

Across967 states, native full-action evaluation prefers A in375, W in143, with
449 ties. The W>A>W0 reversal occurs in43 states/16 games:38 from the old39,
two former native-attack/tie controls and three already-disrupting controls.

Within the **old39 /15 games**, W remains better than A in all39. Removing
future own sabotage reverses that preference in **38/39**, across all15 games.
Thus the old signal is mostly not caused by omitted final construction, but it
also is not a native full-action preference for attacking. Merely replacing
W's forecast by W0 assumes away future actions which can genuinely execute.

### Replanning: some promised attacks slip, many happen on time

The fixed56 cohort starts span23 games and produce **88 primary native-reply
pairs**, plus56 secondary passive-opponent pairs. The separate historical start
adds two native and one passive pair. H is at most eight resolved turns; repeated
starts and replies are correlated and must not be reported as independent games.

Primary promise counts use only the unchanged no-hit arm against its matching
original opponent reply. The first forecast future hit, on that specific region,
is executed on time in **60/88** comparisons. Eleven execute late,13 remain
unfulfilled, and four regions are destroyed by the opponent only after the due
turn:28 missed deadlines across20 starts/12 games.

For the nominated region specifically,14 comparisons make no such promise.
Of the remaining74,47 execute on time,11 late,12 remain unfulfilled and four are
inked by the opponent after the deadline. Within the old39 subgroup there are
64 reply comparisons:12 make no nominated-target promise; **39/52** actual
nominated-target promises execute on time, and **13 miss their deadline** across
**nine starts /six games**. These13 comprise four late hits, seven unfulfilled
hits and two late opponent destructions.

Concrete verified cases, with no extra trajectories added:

- **907134651/t38/P1, target33:** promised for39; refreshed forecasts move it to
  40,41,42; the actual hit and destruction occur42. A destroys it at38. Bounded
  score difference is W=1026, A=1103; both remain nonterminal.
- **907135823/t35/P0, target11:** promise36 moves to37, then the forecast switches
  to region39. Target11 remains intact through42. A destroys11 at35; W=−574,
  A=−438 after the window, both nonterminal.
- **907136694/t73/P1, target45:** under reply0 the promised hit happens on74. Forcing it on73
  worsens reply0 from−3339 to−3528 (−189), while reply1 instead improves by18.
  Under reply1 the target45 promise instead executes on78. Earlier sabotage can
  change both players' subsequent choices in either direction.

Refreshed forecasts expose moving dates/targets but use freshly generated
opponent portfolios. Their values are not validations against the fixed initial
opponent portfolio that advances each primary trajectory.

### Earlier attacks do not reliably improve the bounded outcome

For all88 native reply pairs, A minus W in earned score difference is positive36,
negative19, tied33; median0, range−189..+206. In the old39 subgroup's64 pairs:
**21 positive /14 negative /29 tied**. These are score comparisons, not win rates.

Terminal results W→A in the full primary panel are:

- 70 nonterminal→nonterminal;
- nine nonterminal→loss;
- four win→win;
- four win→nonterminal;
- one loss→loss.

There is **no A-only terminal win within the declared window**. An uncompleted
branch's eventual match outcome remains unknown; the experiment did not extend
the window or run full games.

All six original terminal-win controls in **907134720/P1, starts46,47,49–52**
execute the promised target2 hit exactly on time under native reply0 and win at
the forecast turn. A preserves the win at46/47, but at49–52 leaves the game
nonterminal after eight turns even though its score difference is10 larger.
For start49, W allows own region2 and opposing region15 to ink together on50;
inking2 early changes the opponent's subsequent target to44, leaving the topology
open. This is a concrete reason not to erase all delayed-win continuations or
equate a positive bounded score delta with an improved result.

### Opponent-model dependence and historical case

Against pure WAIT, all56 cohort pairs remain nonterminal: A's bounded score is
higher in35, lower in two, tied in19. Original native first-hit promises execute
on time in14/88 observations, versus60/88 under their matching native replies.
The88 passive observations contain multiple native forecast comparisons per
actual passive trajectory; they are not88 distinct passive games. All six
terminal controls' promises go unfulfilled under passive play. That is a changed
opponent context, not six failures of their original native forecasts.

Historical **904554072/t37/P0**, excluded from cohort totals: complete-action
values remain A=−2044, W=−2012, W0=−2457. With either retained native reply, W
attacks29 on38 as promised and both branches end at−2012, nonterminal. With pure
WAIT, W emits no attack over the window and finishes at−2190; A reaches−1656
(+534), still nonterminal. The old example strongly depends on opponent behavior.

## Decision

**The forecast/execution mismatch is real, but the blanket early-sabotage change
is not supported.** Complete construction retains38/39 old reversals, yet most
named promises execute on time; forced initial hits have mixed score effects,
do not recover terminal wins in this window, and can remove a realized terminal
win from the window or close a losing game sooner.

Keep selected v09 unchanged and close this diagnostic. Do not turn W0's higher
ranking of A into a production selector. A later hypothesis would need to
distinguish repeatedly missed commitments from opponent-dependent, correctly
executed plans, preserving terminal-win and adverse-reply cases. This panel is
now development evidence; any future candidate needs a separately declared change
and the repository practical-v4 strength gate on fresh maps. No candidate or
strength trial was launched as an automatic follow-up.
